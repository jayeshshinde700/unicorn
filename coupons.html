Coupons

Copy a code, then paste it on the booking page to save on your stay.

Loading offers...

How to use a coupon

  1. Copy the code you like, or press Book with this code.
  2. Choose your room and dates, then fill in your details.
  3. On the last step enter the code in Have a coupon? and press Apply. The new price appears straight away.

One code per booking. Conditions are shown on each coupon and are checked again when you pay.
