FAQ

Quick Answers

What are the check-in and check-out times?

Check-in starts at 2:00 PM and check-out is at 11:00 AM. Need to arrive early or stay later? Ask the front desk and we will do our best, subject to availability.

Is parking available?

Yes. Secure on-site parking is free for all in-house guests.

Can you arrange airport transfers?

Yes. Share your flight details when you book, or call the front desk, and we will arrange a pick-up or drop-off.

What is your cancellation policy?

Most direct bookings can be changed or cancelled free of charge up to 48 hours before arrival. Special offers may have their own terms, shown at the time of booking.

Do you host meetings, groups and events?

We do. Pick “Meetings, groups & events” in the form above with your date and guest count, and our team will send a tailored quote.

Which payment methods do you accept?

Major credit and debit cards, UPI and cash at the front desk.
