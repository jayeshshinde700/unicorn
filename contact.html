Reservations & Bookings

Choose a room on our Rooms page and press Reserve, or call our reservations team on +91 99204 00267. The team will confirm availability and your final rate. You can also send us an enquiry using the form on this page.

Please contact our reservations team with your booking details and they will advise you on the changes that are possible.

Availability is confirmed by our reservations team. Call +91 99204 00267, or send your preferred dates and number of guests through the enquiry form and we will get back to you.

Rooms & Amenities

Airport transfers are arranged on advance request through our concierge. Call +91 99204 00267 or send an enquiry, and share your flight details so we can plan the pick-up or drop-off.

Meetings, Events & Weddings

Payments & Policies

We accept major credit and debit cards, UPI and cash at the front desk. Room prices are per room, per night, and taxes are extra.

Cancellation terms can vary by rate or offer. Please ask our reservations team on +91 99204 00267 and they will explain the terms that apply to your booking before you confirm.
