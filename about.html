Our Story

A Stay Designed Around You

At Unicon Hospitality, we believe a great stay is made of small, considered things: a quiet room, a warm welcome, and a team that remembers what matters to you.

Whether you are travelling for business or taking a well-earned break, our focus is on guest satisfaction. We pair comfortable spaces with attentive service, so every part of your visit feels easy and personal.

From the moment you arrive to the moment you leave, we want your time with us to be one you remember and want to repeat.
