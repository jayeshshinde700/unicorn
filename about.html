About Us

Welcome To Our Heritage By Çimen Hotel

Welcome to Heritage by Çimen Hotel, a newly renovated 3-star hotel located in the heart of Alanya, just steps away from the sea and from city center. Our hotel offers a perfect blend of comfort and elegance, featuring 45 sea-view rooms and modern amenities designed for an exceptional stay. With a focus on quality and guest satisfaction, we provide a unique experience that caters to both leisure and business travelers. Discover the renewed charm of Heritage by Çimen Hotel, where unforgettable memories await

Facilities

Hotel Facilities

Rooms and Suites

Varied types of rooms, from Standard to Luxury suites, equipped with essentials like beds, bathroom, Smart TV, Wi-Fi, safe, air conditioning.

24-Hour Security

On-site security personnel and best surveillance. Secure storage and safe for valuables.

Beach

Experience the beauty of our fine sandy beach, where the gently sloping seabed invites you to relax and enjoy the tranquil waters.

Swimming Pool

Immerse yourself in the serene ambiance of our pool area, designed for ultimate relaxation and enjoyment.
