Good to know

Questions Before You Arrive

What are the swimming pool and gym hours?

The pool is open daily from 6:00 AM to 9:00 PM, with towels and lounger service provided. The fitness centre is open 24/7 for guests, and you enter with your room keycard.

Is high-speed Wi-Fi included in the room rate?

Yes. Optical fibre Wi-Fi is complimentary for unlimited devices in all rooms, the public lobbies and the pool terrace.

How do I arrange an airport transfer?

Transfers are arranged on advance request through our Concierge. Call +91 99204 00267 or use the contact page, and share your flight details so we can plan the pick-up.

Is parking free, and do I need to reserve it?

Parking inside the hotel premises is complimentary for all staying guests, with valet assistance on arrival. If you are arriving with a larger vehicle or more than one car, let us know in advance so we can keep space ready.

Do you provide extra rollaway beds or baby cots?

Yes. Extra beds and baby cots can be arranged on request, subject to availability. Please mention it when you book so the room is ready when you arrive.

When is breakfast served, and is room service available?

Buffet breakfast is served from 7:00 AM to 10:30 AM. In-room dining is available 24 hours a day.
