Winter & New Year

Winter Getaway & New Year

Cool, dry weather makes November to March the busiest stretch of the year across India, and Christmas and New Year dates go first. Book two to four months ahead for the best choice of rooms.

  • Widest choice of rooms when you book early
  • Add a New Year’s Eve dinner to any stay
  • Pool and fitness centre open to guests all day
Summer staycation

A Cool Room and a Long Breakfast

April to June is warm and quieter, so rates ease and the pool comes into its own. A cool room, a lazy morning and nowhere you need to be.

  • Swimming pool and fitness centre included
  • Fresh daily breakfast
  • Flexible late check-out on request
Monsoon escape

Rain on the Window, Tea in the Room

June to September is the best-value season to stay: rain on the glass, a warm cup in your hands and fewer crowds. Stay a little longer and settle in.

  • Our best-value season for rooms
  • Tea and coffee in every room
  • Ask about longer-stay rates

Good to know

Questions About Our Rooms

What are the check-in and check-out times?

Check-in is from 2:00 PM and check-out is by 11:00 AM. Early check-in and late check-out are available on request, subject to availability.

Can we add an extra bed for a child?

Extra beds can be arranged on request, subject to availability. For the most space with children, our Family Suite and The Unicon Suite are the best fit.

Are the prices shown per night?

Yes. Prices are per room, per night, and taxes are extra.

How do I book?

Pick a room and press Reserve, or call us on +91 99204 00267. Our reservations team confirms availability and your final rate.

Which payment methods do you accept?

Major credit and debit cards, UPI and cash at the front desk.

Can you arrange airport transfers?

Yes. Pick-ups and drop-offs are arranged on request. Tell us your flight details when you book.
