Essential Guest Information

Opening Hours

  • Restaurant 12:00 PM – midnight
  • Bar 12:00 PM – midnight
  • In-room dining 24 hours

Dress Code

Smart casual. We ask that guests avoid beachwear, sleeveless vests and flip-flops in the evening.

Age Policy

Families are welcome in the restaurant. Alcohol is served only to guests of legal drinking age, and valid photo ID may be requested.

Accessibility

Step-free access to the restaurant. Let us know of any mobility needs when you book and we will set aside a suitable table.

Dietary Needs

Vegetarian dishes are marked on the menu. Please tell your server about allergies or special diets and the kitchen will advise.

Need Help?

Call +91 99204 00267 and our team will gladly help with bookings, groups and special requests.

Good to know

Dining Questions

Do I need to reserve a table?

Walk-ins are welcome, but we recommend booking for evenings, weekends, match days and groups. Use the form above or call +91 99204 00267.

Is the restaurant open to non-hotel guests?

Yes. The bar and restaurant welcome everyone, whether you are staying with us or not.

Do you have vegetarian, vegan or gluten-free options?

Yes. Vegetarian dishes are marked on the menu, and the kitchen can adapt many dishes for vegan and gluten-free diets. Please tell your server when you order.

Can you host a private party or celebration?

Yes. Our VIP lounge is ideal for birthdays, anniversaries and small gatherings. Send us an enquiry with your date and guest count.

Is breakfast served here?

Hotel guests enjoy a daily buffet breakfast, and in-room dining runs around the clock. See our amenities for timings.

Is there a minimum spend or entry fee for events?

Please check with us when you book, as arrangements can change for special events and match days.
